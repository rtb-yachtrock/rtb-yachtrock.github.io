Band Bio

RTB Yacht Rock brings together seasoned Chicago musicians dedicated to faithfully recreating the pristine studio sounds, infectious grooves, and soaring vocal harmonies of the golden age of soft rock.

From timeless radio classics by Michael McDonald, Hall & Oates, and Steely Dan to deep cuts that every true yacht rock aficionado loves, the band delivers an authentic, feel-good live experience guaranteed to get crowds moving.
